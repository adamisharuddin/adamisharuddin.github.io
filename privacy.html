Privacy Policy — Iqra Publisher

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Iqra Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Iqra team (@iqra.signs) to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies.

What data we access, use and store

Accessed: only an OAuth login token for our own YouTube channel, with the single permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). The app does not access any other user's data, and it does not read any YouTube data such as comments, analytics or subscriber lists.

Used: the token is used only to upload our own finished videos, with their titles and descriptions.

Stored: the token is stored encrypted in GitHub Actions secrets. No other data is stored.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose any data to anyone.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

Google API data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, or on request by email, we delete the stored token within 7 days. We keep no other data, so nothing else needs to be deleted.
