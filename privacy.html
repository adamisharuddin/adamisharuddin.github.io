Privacy Policy — Iqra Publisher

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Iqra Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the Iqra team (@iqra.signs) to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

Data we access. Only the login tokens for our own accounts, used to upload videos and captions. The app does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

Storage. Tokens are kept encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets) and used only to post.

Sharing. We don't sell or share any data.

Google API data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting access. We can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact. Email iqra.signs26@gmail.com, or message @iqra.signs on Instagram.
